At the service window · From the studio

Hire me for a day.

I’m Kyle Dahl, the studio’s founder. I come to you and set up a team of AI agents for your work, the same kind this studio works with. Then I support it from a distance, month by month.

  1. Tell me the problem

    Fill in the form below. Within 48 hours I email you back with a quote, or with questions so we can work out what would help.

  2. One day, at your place

    I travel to you and spend a day setting up a small team of AI helpers that draft, check and keep notes for your work: each with a written job, plain notes they remember in, a daily start and close routine, and a rule that nothing goes out without your yes. Then I walk you through using it on your real work. What fits in the day, and the travel cost, are written in the quote. I have a full-time job, so setup days are arranged ahead.

  3. Support each month, from a distance

    After the day I support the setup remotely, month by month, for as long as you want it. I fix what has drifted and add what you now need. What a month covers is written in the quote.

What I can show you: one company so far, the one I co-own. Everything on this site came out of it, and so did the mistakes. I promise a working setup and my support, not a result: no saved hours, no income figures. The guardrails are written rules and the tool’s permission checks, not hard locks. There is no price list. Each job gets its own quote. Quotes, agreements and invoices come from KD Farm and Garden LLC, doing business as Digital Farm and Garden, the company I co-own. I do the work myself. If I am hired full time for AI work, this service ends.
