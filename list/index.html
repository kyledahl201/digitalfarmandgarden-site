Lab

The Greenhouse

Small experiments growing under glass. Tiny tools, sketches and tests. Some sprout, some don't.

The lantern-dust paths on this site

The glow on the dirt paths follows a hand-painted map of where the paths really are. Your browser scatters lantern light along it, rippling out from the porch, so the light only goes where you could walk.

The tap-to-plant garden

Tap the ground anywhere and a seedling sprouts, unfurls a few leaves and sometimes blooms. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. It fades on its own, like a real garden left alone.

Moon wink

Tap the moon. He's been up all night too, so give him a second. If your device asks for less motion, he just smiles instead.

Privacy

Privacy on this site

The short version: this site doesn't collect anything about you.

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ad pixels and no trackers.
  • No forms, no accounts and no sign-ups. Email links open your own mail app.
  • Everything on this site, including the fonts and pictures, comes from this site. Nothing loads from anyone else.
  • The Firefly Hunt remembers which fireflies you've found in your own browser's storage, so you can pick up where you left off. It never leaves your device. Tap Start over in the jar, or clear this site's data in your browser, to forget it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service. We don't receive or use them. GitHub's own privacy statement covers that.

Our apps

Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, linked from its App Store page.

Journal

Why we drew a farm

Most studio websites are a logo, three screenshots and a contact form. We wanted ours to feel like walking up to a place where things are being made.

So we drew a farm. It's a night farm in the foothills, under a lemon moon, with the aurora full of code. Every building is something we're working on. The Workshop holds what's on the bench. The Card House is where our solitaire game lives. The Schoolhouse is for apps made for kids. Little experiments sprout in the Greenhouse, and the Porch is where we sit and talk about all of it.

A farm felt right because that's how this work actually goes. You plant something, tend it every day, and most days nothing seems to happen. Some things never come up. Some come up crooked and you start over. That's why every project here wears a plain tag instead of a launch date: Planted, Growing, Almost ripe or Harvested. We'd rather tell you where things really stand.

The art was made with AI image tools, then picked, cropped and arranged by us, one building at a time. Zoom in far enough and you'll find the porch light on, a dog asleep by the door and an orange cat named Alex keeping watch from the sky.

Our motto is "Bring the color back." To us that means making things that are honest, kind and a little bit magic, and never treating your attention like it's ours to spend.

Thanks for walking the farm. We'll write again when something's ripe.
