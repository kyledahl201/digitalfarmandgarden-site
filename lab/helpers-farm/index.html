A farm for our helpers

One place to see all your AI helpers at once. Each plot is one helper. Green means the work is flowing, and a lantern means it needs you.

This is a sample. The helpers, the tickets and the days below are made up. Nothing you tap is saved or sent anywhere.

  • Green crops: work is flowing
  • Yellow lantern, farmhand waving: needs you
  • Red lantern, flagged gate: blocked
  • Lavender soil: resting

Tap a plot to read its ticket. Answer the lantern, open the gate, then press Next morning to see a new sample day.

What we're building

More and more small teams run part of their work with AI helpers: one answers questions, one keeps the books tidy, one writes, one checks. Each helper lives in its own window. To know how the day is going, you have to open all of them.

We wanted one place to look. So we're building a single home for all of a team's AI helpers, drawn as a farm, because a farm is something anyone can read from across the yard.

How to read the farm

  • One plot per helper. The sign on the fence says what that helper does.
  • Crops show the work. Tall and green means things are moving. Bare lavender soil means the helper is resting.
  • A lantern means you. When a helper needs a decision, it lights a yellow lantern and waves. You answer once and it gets back to work.
  • A flagged gate means stuck. Something outside the farm has to change first, and the farm tells you what.
  • Fireflies carry handoffs. When one helper finishes and passes work along, you see it cross the field.

The brain behind the fence

Under the picture is the plain part: a list of every job, who has it, and what it's waiting on. A foreman helper plans the day, hands out the work and checks it before it reaches you. The goal is that you only walk out to the field when a lantern is lit.

Where it stands

We're trying an early version on our own studio's work. What you see on this page is a demonstration with made-up data, and it isn't something you can buy or sign up for. We'll write about it in Now Brewing as it grows.
